Hope for Brevard

I volunteer with Hope for Brevard, where I help clean cages, organize supplies, and assist with distributing medications. This work has strengthened my compassion, responsibility, and ability to stay calm and focused in fast-paced environments.

Natchez Trace State Park Trailbuilding

I contributed to trailbuilding efforts at Natchez Trace State Park, helping maintain safe and accessible outdoor spaces. This experience taught me teamwork, environmental stewardship, and the value of hands-on community improvement.

Campus Community Support

As a Resident Assistant, I regularly support students through crisis response, community events, and everyday challenges. While not traditional “volunteering,” it reflects my commitment to service and leadership.
