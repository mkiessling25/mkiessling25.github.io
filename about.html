Who I Am

I'm Madison Kiessling — an engineering student, former CAD drafter, resident assistant, community volunteer, and creator. I love designing things, solving problems, and making spaces better for the people around me.

My work blends technical skill with creativity and leadership. Whether I'm building an RC aircraft, drafting a design, organizing a community event, or helping students feel at home, I bring warmth, precision, and a little charm to everything I do.

What I Value

I believe in kindness, curiosity, and creating things that matter. I’m driven by the idea that engineering isn’t just about machines — it’s about people, and how we can make life better through thoughtful design.
